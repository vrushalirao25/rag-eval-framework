# Incident Reporting Policy

## Definition
An incident is any event that compromises the confidentiality, integrity or availability of customer data or of a critical business service.

## Reporting Timelines
Employees must report a suspected incident to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour of detection.
Incidents classified as reportable must be notified to the regulator within 6 hours of classification.

## Severity Levels
Severity 1 covers any exposure of customer data or an outage of a critical service lasting more than 2 hours.
Severity 2 covers a degraded critical service or an internal data exposure.
Severity 3 covers all other incidents.

## Post-Incident Review
A root cause analysis must be completed within 10 business days for every Severity 1 and Severity 2 incident.
Corrective actions from the review must be tracked to closure by the incident owner.
