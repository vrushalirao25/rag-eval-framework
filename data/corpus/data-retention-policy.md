# Data Retention Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to all business units, employees, contractors and third parties that create, process or store records on behalf of Northwind Financial Services. It covers records in every format, including electronic records, paper records, call recordings and data held in AI and analytics systems.

## Retention Periods
Customer transaction records must be retained for 8 years after the account is closed.
Know Your Customer (KYC) documents must be retained for 5 years after the customer relationship ends.
Customer service call recordings must be retained for 2 years from the date of the call.
Logs of prompts and outputs from generative AI systems must be retained for 1 year.

## Secure Deletion
Records must be securely deleted within 30 days after their retention period expires.
Every deletion must be recorded in the certified deletion log, which is owned by the Records Management team.
Deletion of records held by third-party vendors must be confirmed in writing by the vendor.

## Legal Hold
A legal hold overrides the standard retention and deletion schedule.
Only the Legal team can place or release a legal hold.
All active legal holds must be reviewed every quarter.
