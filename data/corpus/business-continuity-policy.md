# Business Continuity Policy

## Recovery Objectives
Critical business services must have a recovery time objective (RTO) of 4 hours.
Critical business services must have a recovery point objective (RPO) of 15 minutes.
Non-critical services must have an RTO of 24 hours.

## Backups
Production data must be backed up every day.
Backups must be encrypted and stored in a separate location from the production systems.
Backups must be retained for 35 days.

## Testing
Disaster recovery plans for critical services must be tested every 6 months.
Backup restoration must be tested every quarter.
The results of each test must be reported to the Operational Risk Committee.

## Plan Ownership
Every critical service must have a named continuity plan owner.
Continuity plans must be reviewed and updated every year, or after any major change to the service.
