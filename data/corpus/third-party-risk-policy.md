# Third-Party Risk Management Policy

## Vendor Classification
A vendor is classified as critical if it processes customer data or supports a critical business service.
All other vendors are classified as standard.

## Due Diligence
Before onboarding, a critical vendor must complete a security assessment and provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certification.
Standard vendors must complete the standard vendor questionnaire before onboarding.

## Ongoing Monitoring
Critical vendors must be reassessed every year.
Standard vendors must be reassessed every 3 years.
A vendor that suffers a security incident must be reassessed within 60 days of the incident.

## Data Location
Customer data must be stored and processed within the country of operation.
Any cross-border transfer of customer data requires prior approval from both the Compliance team and the Data Protection Officer.
