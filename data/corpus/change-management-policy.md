# Change Management Policy

## Change Types
A standard change is a pre-approved, low-risk change that follows a documented procedure.
A normal change requires assessment and approval by the Change Advisory Board.
An emergency change is made to restore a critical service or fix a security vulnerability that is being actively exploited.

## Approvals
Normal changes must be submitted to the Change Advisory Board at least 5 business days before the planned implementation date.
Emergency changes may be approved by the on-call service owner and must be reviewed by the Change Advisory Board within 2 business days after implementation.

## Change Freeze
No normal changes may be made to production systems during the last 3 business days of each financial quarter.
Emergency changes are still permitted during a change freeze.

## Release of AI Models
A new or retrained AI model is treated as a normal change.
Before release, the model must pass its documented golden test set evaluation and have a rollback plan.
