# Customer Data Privacy Policy

## Consent
Explicit customer consent is required before customer data is used for marketing.
A customer's withdrawal of consent must be honoured within 7 days.

## Data Minimisation
Only the customer data that is necessary for a stated purpose may be collected.
Data collected for one purpose must not be reused for an unrelated purpose without fresh consent.

## Access Requests
Customer requests to access their personal data must be answered within 30 days.
The customer's identity must be verified before any personal data is released.

## Data Masking
Personal data must be masked in all non-production environments.
Production data must never be copied to development or test environments without masking.
