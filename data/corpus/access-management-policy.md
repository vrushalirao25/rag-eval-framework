# Access Management Policy

## Access Provisioning
Access to systems must be requested through the access management portal and approved by the employee's line manager.
Access to systems that hold customer data also requires approval from the system owner.
Shared accounts are prohibited for any system that holds customer data.

## Privileged Access
Privileged access must be granted for a maximum of 90 days and then re-approved.
Every privileged session on a production system must be recorded.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all privileged access.

## Access Reviews
Access to systems that hold customer data must be reviewed every quarter by the system owner.
All other access must be reviewed every 6 months.
Access that is not confirmed during a review must be removed within 5 business days.

## Leavers
Access for an employee who leaves the company must be revoked on their last working day.
Access for contractors must expire automatically at the end date of their contract.
